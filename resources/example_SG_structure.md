Hi studious Advocates!

Below is an _example_ 12-week study plan for the Qiskit v2.X Developer Certification exam. Your group can use it, or change it.

The plan is built around one 90-minute group meeting each week. Between meetings, members study and practice on their own ("homework").

**Important note:** Your group knows best what will work for you. In your first meeting, talk about each member's current skill level and goals. Then decide together how your group wants to study.

Furthermore, each group meeting may look like:

1. 10 min: Review of topic content/resources
* From the homework, discuss common mistakes or hard-to-understand concepts.

2. 20 min: Concept lesson
* Review key theory of this week's focus topic, which may include writing explanations, key definitions, and key concepts on a shared group (perhaps virtual) whiteboard. 

3. 30 min: Hands-on coding
* Attempt coding examples and exercises from the topic content, play with code, create your own code examples on the topic. 

4. 20 min: Certification practice questions
* Tackle practice questions that relate to the week's topic from this bank of [Advocate-created practice exams](https://github.com/qiskit-advocate/qiskit-advocate-library/blob/main/advocate-resources/qiskit-cert-study-resources/advocate_created_practice_exams.md). 

5. 10 min: Wrap-up
* Key takeaways
* Homework
* Resources
* Confirm next meeting time and date
